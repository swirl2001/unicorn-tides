    What's a unicorn low tide?

    A Saturday low tide with:

    −1.0 ftor lower. −0.75 ft counts as almost unicorn.
    After noonso you can walk out on the falling tide
    30+ minof light left after the low

    The year's lowest tides fall at dawn in summer and at dusk in winter, so a deep minus tide with good afternoon light is rare. Almost all of them land on November–February afternoons.

    Notes on the data

    Tides

    • NOAA tide predictions for Pillar Point Harbor (station 9414131), the tide station closest to the Half Moon Bay tide pools. Tides along this stretch of coast arrive within minutes of each other.
    • Heights are in feet above mean lower low water (MLLW), the average of each day's lower low tide. A minus tide drops below that, exposing reef that's usually underwater.
    • Tides follow the moon and sun, so predictions are reliable years ahead. That's why this page can list Saturdays into 2030.

    Daylight

    • Sunrise and sunset are worked out for the reef with NOAA's solar formula, accurate to a couple of minutes.
    • A low only counts if it's at noon or later and at least 30 minutes before sunset. With an afternoon low you get about 3 hours of light on the falling tide, plus a little at the bottom.
    • Leave SF is timed so you reach the beach just as the falling tide drops below 0 ft: about 1 hour to drive from San Francisco to the Ritz-Carlton, then 20 minutes down to the beach. That gives you the whole minus tide, usually about an hour and a half before the low.

    Summer foraging tides

    • The 4 lowest tides of each summer (May–August) that fall on a Saturday, drop below 0 ft and come after sunrise.
    • Leave SF gets you to the beach about an hour before the low, or at sunrise if that's later. The tide is already below 0 ft by then.
    • Seaweed harvesting and poke poling need a California fishing license, with daily limits. Taking anything is banned in marine protected areas, including Fitzgerald Marine Reserve, so check the rules for where you go.

    What the forecast can't see

    • Tide predictions don't include swell, wind or storms. A big swell can wash over the reef even on a minus tide, and low pressure can keep the water a little higher than predicted. Check the surf forecast the day before.

    Alerts

    • An email goes out 7 days before and again the day before each qualifying Saturday. The check runs once a day.
